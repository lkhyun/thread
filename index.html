Contrarian Bot

A personal, non-commercial tool operated by a single individual for their own research.

Privacy Policy

This app uses the Threads API only to read public Threads posts that match stock-market keywords.

The text of those posts is sent to the Anthropic Claude API to classify market sentiment. Results and post text are stored only on the operator's personal computer as analysis logs.

The app does not publish posts, does not sell or share data with anyone else, and is not used by anyone other than the operator.
